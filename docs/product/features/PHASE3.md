# Phase 3: Advanced and Large-Scale CRM

**Goal:** Support more complex organizations, larger deployments, and broader customer engagement after the earlier phases demonstrate demand and operational readiness.

## Advanced sales and governance

- Territory management, lead scoring and routing, and service-level rules.
- Approval workflows and advanced authorization, including field-level controls.
- Sandbox environments and metadata-driven custom objects.

## Customer engagement and ecosystem

- Marketplace and integrations for communication, telephony, and accounting tools.
- Customer service capabilities such as ticketing and a knowledge base.
- Marketing automation and audience segmentation.
- AI-assisted capabilities such as lead scoring, email drafting, summaries, and next-step suggestions.

## Enterprise and analytics

- Data warehouse and business-intelligence exports with advanced analytics.
- Single sign-on using SAML or OIDC and user provisioning with SCIM.
- Data residency controls and per-tenant encryption keys where required.

## Readiness criteria

Treat these as discovery candidates, not a delivery commitment. Each capability needs validated customer demand, a security and privacy review, clear operational ownership, and an approach that fits the tenant-isolation model.
