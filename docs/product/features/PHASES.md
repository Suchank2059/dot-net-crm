# Feature Roadmap

The phases describe a possible progression, not a fixed delivery schedule. Prioritize individual capabilities based on customer needs, dependencies, and operational readiness.

| Phase | Focus | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phase 1 | Secure MVP CRM workflows for small teams | [Phase 1: MVP CRM](./PHASE1.md) |
| Phase 2 | Team configuration, integrations, and automation | [Phase 2: Growing CRM](./PHASE2.md) |
| Phase 3 | Advanced governance, ecosystem, and scale | [Phase 3: Advanced CRM](./PHASE3.md) |

Phase 1 establishes the CRM records, sales process, and tenant access model. Later phases build on that foundation; they should not be treated as commitments until validated.
